# 🎒 Decathlon Shopping List — Summer 2026 Trip

Solo budget trip across **Kyrgyzstan → Kazakhstan → Azerbaijan → Georgia → Armenia → Turkey**, ~40 days, late May → early July.

**Climate range to cover**: Song-Kul yurt at 0°C (May 30) → Aktau steppe at 35°C (June 10) → Black Sea coast in Rize (humid + rain).

---

## 👖 Layer 1: The Pants (Most Important Single Item)

### 🥇 Quechua MH500 Modular Hiking Trousers (Zip-Off)
- 🇹🇷 [decathlon.com.tr — MH500 Modular](https://www.decathlon.com.tr/p/erkek-outdoor-moduler-pantolon-mh500/_/R-p-325457) — ~₺1,200–1,500
- 🇬🇧 [decathlon.co.uk — MH500](https://www.decathlon.co.uk/p/men%27s-modular-hiking-trousers-mh500-beige/_/R-p-325457) — ~£25–30

**Features:**
- ✂️ Zips off to shorts in seconds (different coloured zips for left/right)
- 🔄 Two-way stretch (15% elastane) — flexible for hiking + presentable in cities
- 💨 Quick-dry, lightweight (~300g in size L)
- 🧳 Four zipped pockets (2 hand, 1 back, 1 thigh)
- 🎯 **Use**: Song-Kul (full trousers) → Ala-Archa (full) → Almaty city (full) → Aktau/Baku (zip to shorts) → Rize hiking (full)

**Why this single item works for the whole trip**: The temperature range (5°C to 35°C) demands either zip-off pants or *two separate garments*. One zip-off pair = half the packing weight.

### 🥇 Forclaz MT100 Hooded Synthetic Puffer Jacket (-5°C rated)
- 🇹🇷 [decathlon.com.tr — MT100 -5°C kapüşonlu şişme mont](https://www.decathlon.com.tr/p/erkek-outdoor-trekking-kapusonlu-sisme-mont-sentetik-haki-mt100-5degc/_/R-p-312478) — **~₺1,990**
- 🇬🇧 [decathlon.co.uk — MT100 Hooded Puffer](https://www.decathlon.co.uk/sports/hiking/men-s-puffer-jackets-and-down-jackets) — ~£35–45

**Features:**
- 🪶 Lightweight (~280g)
- 🔄 Packs into its own pocket — size of a grapefruit
- 💧 Synthetic fill works when damp (unlike down) — critical for humid Rize
- 🏔️ Comfort range: -5°C to +5°C (perfect for cold mornings, alpine breaks, yurt sleeping)
- 🎯 **Use**: Song-Kul evenings + sleeping, all alpine breaks, cool early mornings everywhere, Rize evening rain

> 💡 **Why not a fleece?** Same warmth needs 3× the packing space. Bad ROI for a long trip.

## 🚨 Essentials You'll Wish You Packed

These are the items every long-trip traveller forgets and then regrets.

| Item | Why It's Critical | ~TL Price |
|------|-------------------|-----------|
| 🔦 **[Headlamp](https://www.decathlon.com.tr/tum-sporlar/hiking-trekking-outdoor/fenerler)** (Forclaz HL100) | **No electricity in Song-Kul yurt at night** — both hands free. Also marshrutka night stops, hostel searching | ~₺250–500 |
| 🧴 **Sunscreen SPF 50+** | High UV at Song-Kul (3,016m), Caspian glare, Aktau steppe sun. Local sunscreen in KG/KZ is poor | ~₺250 |
| 💋 **Lip balm with SPF** | Altitude + dry air = cracked lips guaranteed | ~₺50–100 (pharmacy) |

| 🦟 **Mosquito repellent (DEET 30%+)** | Essential for Batumi + Rize (humid Black Sea) — Lake Sevan + Issyk-Kul shore at dusk | ~₺150 (eczane) |
| 🧦 **Hiking socks ×3** | One thicker + two thinner. Bad socks = blisters at Ala-Archa | ~₺150/pair |
| 🩲 **Quick-dry travel underwear ×3** | Synthetic dries overnight when you wash. Cotton ruins long marshrutka days | ~₺150/pair |
| 👕 **Quick-dry t-shirts ×3** (synthetic or merino) | Cotton stays damp in humid Rize. Merino wears 4–5 days without smelling | ~₺300–800/each |

| 🧰 **Padlock (TSA combination)** | Hostel lockers + securing your bag in marshrutka cargo hold | ~₺200 |
| 💧 **Reusable water bottle 1L** | Save money + free *pulpulak* fountains in Yerevan, tap-safe Tbilisi | ~₺200 |
| 🌂 **Microfibre travel towel** | Hostels often charge; useful for hammam, beach | ~₺300 |
| 🩺 **Small first-aid kit** | Plasters, blister patches, paracetamol, Imodium, electrolyte sachets | ~₺250 (eczane) |
| 🎒 **20L day pack** (Quechua NH100) | Day trips while big bag stays at hotel | ~₺400 |
| 🎩 **Beanie** + thin liner gloves | Song-Kul morning is cold | ~₺200 total |

## ⛔ Don't Buy (Common Overpacking Mistakes)

| Item | Why Not |
|------|---------|
| Heavy parka | Way too warm for summer trip — MT100 puffy is enough |
| Multiple fleeces | Puffy + base layer is warmer + lighter |
| Heavy hiking boots | MH100 / trail runners handle every hike |
| Hard-shell waterproof trousers | MH500 dries fast enough |
| Sleeping bag | Yurts and guesthouses provide bedding |
| Hydration bladder | Bottle is fine + simpler to refill |
| Travel pillow | Stuff your puffy into its hood — instant pillow |
| Multiple shorts | Olaian 500 doubles as daily shorts |
| Dehydrated food / camping stove | Every guesthouse and tour includes meals |

## 🎒 How the Layers Combine

| Scenario | What you wear |
|---|---|
| 🏔️ **Song-Kul yurt sleep** (0°C inside) | Base layer top + bottom + socks + beanie + puffy + provided blankets |
| 🥾 **Ala-Archa hiking up** (10°C, working hard) | T-shirt + MH500 pants |
| 🥾 **Ala-Archa hiking break** (cold, stopped) | Add puffy |
| 🥾 **Ala-Archa storm** | Add rain shell over puffy |
| 🌞 **Aktau / Baku** (35°C) | T-shirt + MH500 unzipped to shorts (or Olaian) |
| 🍽️ **Tbilisi restaurant evening** | T-shirt + zip-off as full pants |
| 🌧️ **Rize rain** | Rain shell + MH500 pants |
| 🛁 **Hammam / hot springs** | Olaian 500 |
| 🌊 **Batumi beach** | Olaian 500 + flip-flops |

One outfit transforms into 9 use cases. That's why the list is small but specific.
