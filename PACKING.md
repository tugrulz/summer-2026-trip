# 🎒 Decathlon Shopping List — Summer 2026 Trip

Solo budget trip across **Kyrgyzstan → Kazakhstan → Azerbaijan → Georgia → Armenia → Turkey**, ~40 days, late May → early July.

**Climate range to cover**: Song-Kul yurt at 0°C (May 30) → Aktau steppe at 35°C (June 10) → Black Sea coast in Rize (humid + rain).

Total budget for this list: **~£150–200**. Total weight: **~1.5 kg**.

---

## 👖 Layer 1: The Pants (Most Important Single Item)

### 🥇 [Quechua MH500 Modular Hiking Trousers (Zip-Off)](https://www.decathlon.co.uk/p/men%27s-modular-hiking-trousers-mh500-beige/_/R-p-325457) — **~£25–30**

- ✂️ Zips off to shorts in seconds (different coloured zips for left/right)
- 🔄 Two-way stretch (15% elastane) — flexible for hiking + presentable in cities
- 💨 Quick-dry, lightweight
- 🧳 Four zipped pockets (2 hand, 1 back, 1 thigh)
- 🎯 **Use**: Song-Kul (full trousers) → Ala-Archa (full) → Almaty city (full) → Aktau/Baku (zip to shorts) → Rize hiking (full)

**Why this single item works for the whole trip**: The temperature range (5°C to 35°C) demands either zip-off pants or *two separate garments*. One zip-off pair = half the packing weight.

---

## 🧥 Layer 2: The Puffy (Single Best Piece of Gear)

### 🥇 [Forclaz MT100 Hooded Synthetic Puffer Jacket](https://www.decathlon.co.uk/sports/hiking/men-s-puffer-jackets-and-down-jackets) — **~£35–45**

- 🪶 Lightweight (~280g)
- 🔄 Packs into its own pocket — size of a grapefruit
- 💧 Synthetic fill works when damp (unlike down) — critical for humid Rize
- 🏔️ Warmth: cold yurt mornings, Ala-Archa glacier wind, Shymbulak cable car top
- 🎯 **Use**: Song-Kul evenings + sleeping, all alpine breaks, cool early mornings everywhere, Rize evening rain

> 💡 **Why not a fleece?** Same warmth needs 3× the packing space. Bad ROI for a long trip.

---

## 🌧️ Layer 3: Rain Shell

### 🥇 [Quechua MH100 Waterproof Hiking Jacket](https://www.decathlon.co.uk/p/men-s-mh100-waterproof-mountain-walking-jacket/_/R-p-310506) — **~£20–30**

- 💧 Fully waterproof (not just water-repellent)
- 🪶 Light (~250g)
- 🔄 Packs small
- 🎯 **Use**: Ala-Archa afternoon storms, Rize tea-belt rain, marshrutka days, surprise summer storms

> ⚠️ Don't substitute with a windbreaker — Rize gets actual rain.

### [Forclaz MT100 Merino Wool Base Layer Bottom (Long John)](https://www.decathlon.co.uk/sports/hiking) — **~£20**

## 🩳 Layer 4: Hybrid Swim Shorts (Wear-Anywhere)

Need to function as both **swimwear** and **daily city shorts**.

### 🥇 [Olaian 500 Standard Boardshorts](https://www.decathlon.co.uk/p/surfing-standard-boardshorts-500-gradient/_/R-p-310625) — **~£15–20**

- 🔒 **Zipped side pocket** — phone-safe at the beach, but also useful in the city
- 💧 Water-repellent + quick-dry (dries in ~30 min in sun)
- 🔄 2-way stretch
- 📏 Mid-thigh length — passes for normal shorts, not "obvious swimwear"
- 🎨 **Buy a plain colour** (navy / black / khaki) — printed surfer designs scream "beach", plain colours pass as shorts in any restaurant
- 🎯 **Used at**: Altyn Arashan hot springs → Caspian Sea → Lake Sevan → Batumi → Ayder thermal pools → Black Sea Rize/Trabzon → as daily shorts in hot cities

> ⚠️ Avoid the **Olaian 100** (cheaper but obvious-swimwear cut) and the **Olaian Long** (board-shorty, less versatile in a restaurant).

## ⛔ Don't Buy (Common Over-Packing Mistakes)

| Item | Why Not |
|---|---|
| Heavy parka | Way too warm for summer trip |
| Multiple fleeces | Puffy + base layer is warmer + lighter |
| Heavy hiking boots | Trail runners handle Ala-Archa and Sümela hikes fine |
| Hard-shell waterproof trousers | Overkill — your MH500 dries fast enough |
| Convertible sandals | Flip-flops are simpler |
| Hydration bladder | Bottle is fine, simpler to refill at pulpulak fountains in Yerevan + tap in Tbilisi |
| Travel pillow | Stuff your puffy into its hood — instant pillow |
| Multiple shorts | Olaian 500 doubles as daily shorts |
| Specialised water shoes | Crocs/flip-flops for pebble beaches |
